The Challenge

Anthropometry measurement
doesn't have to be hard

Time Consuming

Manual measurement of hundreds of students takes days or even weeks.

Error Prone

No Insights

Our Solution

One integrated system
for the whole school

MySimoka SmartGrowth combines a smart measurement device, Android app, and cloud analytics to make anthropometry recording fast, accurate, and actionable. Identify students with face recognition, record automatically, and get instant reports — all in one seamless workflow.
